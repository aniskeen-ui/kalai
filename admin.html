Reports

Paid orders only (Paid, Being framed, Shipped, Delivered, Replacement, Refunded), by order date. Planned costs are the estimates saved in each order's Costing box; actual costs come from the bills entered under Actual costs on each paid order (a cost with no bill yet stays at its planned figure, shown in italics). Both include the actual extra cost of any replacement (less insurance received); refunds and cancelled Siva orders are taken into account. Sales are shown with VAT; margin is on net sales (excluding VAT and the payment fee).

Works go live on their date automatically (09:00 Dubai), newest first. Edits save as you type out of a field. Sold works stay on the website marked “Sold”; hidden ones don't show. Prices are set only in the Pricing tab; each work shows the price the website shows now.

Pricing

Fixed website prices for each artwork: the artist's price (he gives it under "My works & prices") + courier India → Dubai + customs + frame work + frame (by style) + margin + card charges + courier Dubai → customer. Change any input to simulate, compare with the market, then approve: the prices show on the website.

Customers

One entry per customer (BP-…), kept up to date from their orders. Change a customer here, or they change their own details on the website (My details): the change goes to their open orders too.

Settings

Website timings and home page texts. Changes apply the next time a visitor opens the website.

Offers

Open Offers page

Each offer can show a banner across the top of the website, appear on the Offers page (art.pranixtechno.com/offers.html, easy to share), and carry a discount code that customers type on their order.

Artists & sellers

Artist & seller portalSupplier portal

Artists (“Become an artist” on the website, with sample works) and craft sellers apply on the portal. You approve them with a commission % (added on top of their price) and a delivery fee for orders Kalai collects and delivers. Each listing needs your approval before it shows on the website.

Thirukkural of the day

Fully automatic: every day the website puts up the next Thirukkural (Aram and Porul) in Tamil, English and Arabic, at the hour set in Settings → Timings. You don't need to type anything here.
