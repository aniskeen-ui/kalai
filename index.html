Delivery & questions

Will I pay customs at the door?

No. Every price includes UAE customs duty and VAT, and we ship duties paid, so the courier hands it over with nothing to collect.

How long does delivery take?

Ready-to-ship works arrive in 5–8 days anywhere in the UAE. Commissions take 1–2 weeks for pencil sketches and 2–3 weeks for watercolours, plus delivery.

What materials do you use?

Watercolours are painted on 300 gsm cotton paper with artist-grade paints. Pencil sketches use graphite on heavy acid-free drawing paper, sealed with fixative so they don't smudge.

What if it arrives damaged?

Every parcel is insured. Report it under My enquiries & orders with photos within 48 hours of delivery and choose: a replacement or repair, or a full refund. Because each work is one of a kind, we can't accept change-of-mind returns.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, under My enquiries & orders on this site, where you see the refund before you confirm. Before you pay, there's nothing to pay. After payment, if the artist hasn't started your work, you get a full refund. Once the artist has started (including the pencil sketch for commissions), we refund 50% to cover the artist's time. Once it has shipped it can't be cancelled.

What does Little Artists cost?

For children aged 3 to 10, framing is free and you pay only delivery: ⃃ 25 within Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman, AED 35 to the other emirates, or the overseas shipping charge for your country (shown on the form; confirmed with your payment link). One free framing per child each month. For ages 11 to 17, framing costs ⃃ 79 plus delivery. Premium frames, A3 size, extra copies and gift wrap are optional extras at any age.

Do you deliver outside the UAE?

Yes, we deliver worldwide. Within the UAE, delivery is included in the price. For any other country, we confirm the shipping charge with your payment link, before you pay. Import duty or taxes charged by your country, if any, are paid on arrival.

Can I visit to see paintings?

We are an online gallery and paintings ship directly from our artists' studios. For larger commissions we can arrange a video call with the artist.
