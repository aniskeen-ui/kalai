AI made it perfect. Your child made it priceless.

Look at the two parrots. The one on the right is neat and polished. It was made by clever software, trained by brilliant researchers on millions of pictures, in a few seconds. It can be made again, a thousand times, exactly the same.

The one on the left was made by a little hand holding a brush that felt too big. The beak went outside the lines. The red ran into the blue. And somewhere in the middle, there was a moment of pure concentration, maybe a tongue poking out, maybe a proud call of "Look what I made!"

That painting can never be made again. Not because the colours were special, but because your child will never be this age again. Next year their hand will be steadier, their parrots more careful. This one is a snapshot of who they are right now.

Questions parents ask

Good to know

How old can my child be?

Little Artists is for children aged 3 to 17. For ages 3 to 10 the framing is free; for ages 11 to 17 there is a small framing fee.

Do I need to send the original drawing?

No. A clear photo taken in daylight, straight above the paper, is usually enough. If the photo is too small to print sharply, we will ask you to send the paper drawing.

Can it be sent to grandparents in another country?

Yes. Within the UAE delivery is a fixed small charge; for other countries we confirm the shipping charge before you pay.

Will my child's drawing appear on your website?

Never without your permission. The children shown on our site are illustrations, and any real drawing we show is shared with the family's consent.
